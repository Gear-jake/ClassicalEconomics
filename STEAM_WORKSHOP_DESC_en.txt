[h1]Classical Economics[/h1]
[b]A macroeconomics & nation-governance simulation mod for WorldBox ｜ Current version 1.4.0[/b]
[b]Requires:[/b] WorldBox 0.51.2+ and NeoModLoader (NML)

Adds a full macroeconomic and **national-governance** simulation to WorldBox. Every race that can found a kingdom (Fire/Ice Demons, aliens...) joins the economy; play one nation yourself or watch the AI kingdoms govern themselves.

[list]
[*][b]Macroeconomics:[/b] wealth tracking for all races - GDP, average wealth, Gini coefficient, CPI per kingdom; Boom -> Recession -> Depression -> Recovery cycle, bubble bursts, famine and inflation
[*][b]Economic systems:[/b] labor wages, banking credit with default contagion in depressions (spreading to weaker kingdoms), disaster shocks (volcano/meteor), inheritance (parents -> spouse -> children, or split by damage dealt)
[*][b]Social unrest:[/b] inequality triggers rebellion -> street uprising -> revolution; war plunder and dynasty monitoring
[*][b][Playable Government - Central Banker][/b] Hover a kingdom on the map and press G (rebindable) to claim it: royal treasury (founding grant + periodic levies), 6 ongoing policies x 3 tiers with slots (coinage / state monopoly / propaganda / poor relief...), one-shot decrees (relief / festival), **vanilla building placement** (houses/barracks/watch tower/well and more - 10 kinds, click the map to build), track record and a nation GDP trend chart, and **diplomacy** (declare war / sue for peace / alliances / gifts / bilateral economic pacts - a two-level page: nation list -> detail with a three-layer crest and full stats); **the Central Banker fully persists**: treasury, claim, policies, buildings, track record, diplomacy and the GDP chart history are all saved and restored on load
[*][b][Kingdom Decision Events][/b] **16 events across 6 families** (finance/disaster/court/military/civil/diplomacy): condition-filtered yearly draw; your kingdom gets a **non-modal choice window** (the cautious option runs on timeout; a Cabinet to-do entry), AI kingdoms **decide by national character** and land in the event feed; **rebuilt event window**: filter chips + fold-by-year + single-column timeline, an always-open window rebuilds once per year; frequency configurable, event state persists in saves
[*][b][National Law System - Codex][/b] **Every kingdom (AI included): 28 laws x 5 tiers + 16 policies x 3 tiers** - evolving yearly from the national situation (finance/Gini/war/population/disaster) plus 6 national natures; mutual-exclusion law pairs, paid upgrades and free downgrades, all persisted in saves; effects genuinely wired into 9 engines (production/price/Gini/unrest/consumption/disaster/build cost/wages/military) and shown on **citizen traits** (Scholar State, Militarized, etc.); the law page shows a live aggregate-effect summary and semantic tier names (e.g. Commercial Policy: laissez-faire -> command economy)
[*][b]UI:[/b] hub-style Cabinet with five tabs (Finance / Policies / Decrees & Build / Diplomacy / Laws, 600x760, buttons adapt to window resizing); **the Cabinet refreshes itself after each yearly settlement**; **an adjustable UI scale for fonts and buttons (0.8-1.6x, applies instantly from settings)**; slate-and-gold 9-slice UI; floating windows for overview/trends/events/rich list
[/list]

[b]Performance:[/b] frame-budgeted yearly settlement (4 ms/frame, 2 s window; only spending/banking are reduced when over budget - taxes are never reduced), automatic memory cleanup, background-thread statistics (no pathfinding on the main thread), 42 automated gates, fully conserved gold.

[b]Localization:[/b] four languages (简体 / 繁體 / English / Русский), the whole UI switches instantly; citizens carry visible codex traits.

[b]Installation:[/b] Install NML first, put the EconomyMod folder into WorldBox/Mods/, enable in-game.
[b]Controls:[/b] hover a kingdom and press G to claim / open the Cabinet (rebindable in settings); a ledger button sits in the top-right of nation/city windows.

[b]Compatibility:[/b] built entirely on vanilla mechanics, no external assets, compatible with performance mods such as Optime; mod state is stored in kingdom data keys (codex rb_law_*, central banker rb_nat_*), so it saves with your game - older saves (1.2.1fix and earlier) load directly with no law-tier shift.
