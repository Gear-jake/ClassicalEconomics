[h1]Classical Economics[/h1]
[b]A macroeconomic simulation mod for WorldBox | v1.0.0[/b][b]Requires:[/b] WorldBox 0.51.2+ and NeoModLoader (NML)

Gives your world a real economy. The mod tracks the wealth of every creature that has built a city or belongs to a kingdom (including aliens, dragons, Fire Demons and Ice Demons), then simulates a full macroeconomic system on top of it:

[list]
[*]Macro statistics: global GDP, average wealth, Gini coefficient, price index (CPI), per-kingdom breakdown
[*]Economic cycle: Boom / Recession / Depression / Recovery with bubble buildup and bursts; famine in depression
[*]Unrest & revolution: inequality triggers rebellions, uprisings and revolutions; war plunder transfers loot to winners
[*]Kingdom trade: zero-sum trade flows (surplus = deficit, no coins created from nothing) + biome specialties for comparative-advantage trade; [b]geographic trade[/b] — distance decay between capitals, transport costs, and regional price arbitrage; [b]adaptive trade parameters (v0.11)[/b] — distance decay / transport cost / arbitrage weight are derived each cycle from game state, no manual config needed
[*][b]Net trade ranking (v0.13)[/b]: a floating window ranks cities/kingdoms by net trade (exports − imports), surplus green / deficit red; a kingdom's value = sum of its cities
[*][b]Trade military power (v0.13)[/b]: surplus nations' people gain damage/armor, deficit nations suffer damage — trade powerhouses fight stronger
[*]Labor division: employed population creates wages through a production function
[*]Sapient spending: the rich spend yearly in 7 ways (weapons, building investment, arsenal, wholesale, era events, charity, taxes) — coins never vanish
[*]Era events: Golden Age / Revival / Flourishing / Economic Collapse
[*]Banking credit: the rich lend to the poor; defaults soar in depressions; crises spread along trade routes
[*]Disaster shocks: volcanoes and meteors evaporate affected city wealth; volcanoes temporarily boost mining
[*]Inheritance: wealth passes parents → spouse → children; mayors collect inheritance tax
[*]Policy & governance: redistribution, fiscal policy, tariffs; failed rulers abdicate
[/list]

[b]UI:[/b] Economy tab in the toolbar with 7 buttons; overview panel, wealth & inequality trend charts, event stream window (major events are kept permanently, never pushed out by frequent ones).

[b]Installation:[/b] Install NML first, then put the EconomyMod folder into WorldBox/Mods/ and enable it in-game.

[b]Compatibility:[/b] Built entirely on vanilla mechanics, no external resources; all statistics computed on a background thread (zero main-thread cost). Fully compatible with the Optime optimization mod — the compatibility layer injects null-guards at runtime and never modifies Optime.

UI languages: 简体中文 / 繁體中文 / English / Русский

[b]v1.0.0 new content[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Central Banker gameplay[/b] Claim a kingdom and serve as its economy minister: a real royal treasury (levies and spending), 6 ongoing policies (tax cuts/hikes, poor relief, propaganda, trade pacts, tariffs; 3-tier slot system), emergency relief & national festival decrees, market & granary buildings on the map (destroyed by wars and disasters)
[*][b]Diplomacy[/b] Declare war, sue for peace (pay ransom when weaker), form alliances, send gifts, and sign bilateral trade pacts (+10% bilateral flow per tier) - all effective in real time

[*][b]Performance[/b] Frame-budgeted annual settlement, automatic memory cleanup, real A* trade-path costs (detours around mountains and seas)
[*][b]Full localization[/b] Key sets identical across Simplified/Traditional Chinese, English and Russian; the whole UI switches language instantly
[/list]
